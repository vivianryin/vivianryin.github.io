🦁 Leading Change at Columbia as President of the Applied Analytics Club

In May 2024, I was elected as the president of Columbia Applied Analytics Club (APAN Club). APAN Club is the largest student organization in Columbia University's School of Professional Studies with over 1000 active members. As the President of APAN Club, I led a dynamic board team of 30+ students to foster a thriving community of aspiring analytics professionals. Under my leadership, the club has achieved significant milestones:

  • Increased membership by 20% and enhanced member engagement by 40%, creating a more vibrant and active club community.
  • Secured over $5,000 in sponsorships and generated $1,000 in ticket revenue to fund a variety of innovative club initiatives.
  • Hosted over 20 high-impact event, including professional development panel, diverse networking sessions, attracting over 2000 attendees.
  • Implemented a structured attendance tracking system, reducing the event no-show rate by over 50% and allowing accurate budget allocation.
  • Built strong relationships with entrepreneurs, professionals, and student communities to unlock exclusive opportunities

Professional Development Panels

Hiring Manager Panel

Oct 9th, 2024 – Knowing how intimidating the hiring process can be for students, I reached out to YouTube content creator Christine Jiang, also a former data director and hiring manager, for a special hiring manager panel. Offering students with insider tips on what hiring managers are looking for on resumes, and how to navigate the data job market.

This panel helped attendees feel more prepared to stand out in competitive hiring environments, and the panel deck has over 1000 views.

Internship Panels

Sept 11th, 2024 – I designed this panel to help bridge the gap for students navigating the often daunting internship search process.

Recognizing the anxiety, I invited returning students with diverse internship experiences to share their journey, equipping new students with practical advice to tackle their internship search. excelling in internships, and leveraging those opportunities for long-term career growth.

Over 150 Students attended the Internship Panel, and rated 5/5 by participants.
